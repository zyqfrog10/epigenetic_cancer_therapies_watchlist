# Epigenetic Cancer Therapies Watchlist

A quarterly-maintained, source-cited landscape tracker of **approved, withdrawn, and emerging epigenetic therapies in oncology**, spanning small-molecule chromatin modulators and DNA-cut-free epigenetic editing platforms.

**Last updated:** November 20, 2026  
**Update cadence:** Quarterly (February, May, August, November)

---

## Snapshot (November 2026)

- **12 active U.S. FDA-approved epigenetic cancer therapies** across four classes: DNMT inhibitors (3), HDAC inhibitors (3), mutant IDH1/2 inhibitors (4), and menin inhibitors (2), plus **2 regional ex-U.S. approvals** ([valemetostat](https://www.daiichisankyo.com/files/news/pressrelease/pdf/202209/20220926_E.pdf) in Japan; [tucidinostat](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/17501911.2024.2430169) in China).
- **Biomarker-gated drugs now represent half of active U.S. approvals**, led by Servier's brain-penetrant dual IDH1/2 inhibitor [vorasidenib](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-vorasidenib-grade-2-astrocytoma-or-oligodendroglioma-susceptible-idh1-or-idh2-mutation) and back-to-back menin inhibitor clearances for Syndax's [revumenib](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-revumenib-relapsed-or-refractory-acute-myeloid-leukemia-susceptible-npm1-mutation) and Kura Oncology's [ziftomenib](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/cancer-approvals/fda-approves-ziftomenib-relapsed-or-refractory-acute-myeloid-leukemia-npm1-mutation).
- **Zero active U.S. EZH2 approvals** following [Ipsen's March 2026 global voluntary withdrawal of Tazverik (tazemetostat)](https://www.ipsen.com/press-release/ipsen-voluntarily-withdraws-tazverik-tazemetostat-in-follicular-lymphoma-and-epithelioid-sarcoma-3251503/) due to secondary hematologic malignancy risk ([FDA alert](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-alerts-and-statements/fda-alerts-health-care-providers-and-patients-about-increased-risk-new-blood-cancers-tazverik)).
- **Solid-tumor synthetic lethality & chromatin writers lead the emerging pipeline**, anchored by MTA-cooperative PRMT5 inhibitors in *MTAP*-deleted tumors ([Tango's vopimetostat](https://tangotx.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/tango-therapeutics-reports-positive-data-ongoing-phase-12-study), [BMS-986504](https://www.iaslc.org/iaslc-news/press-release/bms-986504-demonstrates-durable-responses-mtap-deleted-nsclc-including)), Pfizer's Phase 3 KAT6 inhibitor [prifetrastat](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07062965) in ER+/HER2− breast cancer, and [Oryzon's iadademstat](https://www.oryzon.com/en/news-events/news/oryzon-present-updated-positive-clinical-data-iadademstat-hematological) + [Merck's bomedemstat](https://www.msdclinicaltrials.com/trial/nct07722611/) in myeloid malignancies.

## Key Pipeline Themes Tracked

1. **Biomarker-Gated Small Molecules Over Broad Reprogramming:** How genetic selection (*IDH1/2*, *KMT2A*, *NPM1*, *MTAP* deletion) is overcoming the narrow therapeutic windows that limited first-generation DNMT and pan-HDAC inhibitors ([Frontiers in Pharmacology, 2026](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/pharmacology/articles/10.3389/fphar.2026.1965981/full)).
2. **Next-Generation Combinations & Safety Monitoring:** Triplet regimens in AML (LSD1 or menin inhibitors + azacitidine/venetoclax) and long-term secondary malignancy surveillance after the tazemetostat withdrawal and [Foghorn/Lilly's October 2026 discontinuation of FHD-909](https://ir.foghorntx.com/news-releases/news-release-details/foghorn-therapeutics-provides-update-fhd-909-and-strategic).
3. **DNA-Cut-Free Epigenetic Editing:** Clinical translation of dCas9 and zinc-finger epigenetic silencers/activators from [Tune Therapeutics](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/tune-series-b-epigenetic-editing-hepatitis-b/737052/), [nChroma Bio](https://www.nchromabio.com/), [Epicrispr Biotechnologies](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/epicrispr-series-c-epigenetic-editing-fshd/827340/), and [Moonwalk Biosciences](https://www.biospace.com/moonwalk-biosciences-to-present-data-showcasing-epiread-and-epiwrite-next-generation-multiplex-epigenetic-engineering-platform-at-asgct-2024), including multiplex CAR-T engineering for solid tumors.
